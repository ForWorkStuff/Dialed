Use Dialed on your phone

Your phone and this computer need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

Checking\u2026

Add it to your home screen (optional)

  • iPhone: open the address in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open it in Chrome or Brave, tap the \u22EE menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).

Good to know

  • Keep the screen on with Dialed in front: YouTube pauses its player when a phone's screen locks. Dialed asks the phone to stay awake while music plays.
  • iPhone web pages can't change volume, so there is no crossfade there: songs play back to back and the DJ talks between them.
  • Phone mode lets other devices on your Wi-Fi reach Dialed. Use it on networks you trust, and close the Dialed window when you're done.
